Hormone replacement therapy

Bioidentical hormones · Lab-guided dosing · Regular monitoring

Hormone therapy tailored to your labs and symptoms, adjusted over time as your body changes.

  • MenopauseHot flashes, sleep disruption, low libido, and mood changes during perimenopause and menopause.
  • AndropauseLow energy, low libido, and loss of strength linked to falling testosterone in men.
  • Adrenal fatiguePersistent tiredness and stress intolerance, evaluated with a full hormone picture.
  • FibromyalgiaWidespread pain and unrefreshing sleep, with attention to hormonal contributors.
  • PCOSCycle regulation, metabolic health, and symptoms of Polycystic Ovary Syndrome.

Weight management

Metabolic testing · Medication options · Ongoing support

Medically supervised weight management that looks at the hormonal and metabolic causes of weight gain, not just calories. Your plan may include lab work, prescription options where appropriate, and regular check-ins.
