Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information EternaWell Medicine ("we") collects through this website and how we use it. It covers the website only; information you give us as a patient during care is also protected as described in our Notice of Privacy Practices.

What we collect

  • Online booking: your name, mobile phone, email, the visit type you choose, and the date and time you book.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you subscribe.
  • Technical data: our web host and font provider receive standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) when pages load. We do not use advertising trackers or sell any data.

How we use it

  • To schedule, confirm, remind you about, reschedule, or cancel your appointment.
  • To contact you about your appointment by phone, text, or email.
  • To send newsletter emails you asked for. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that run this website and our scheduling for us, such as our website host, our calendar and email provider, and our patient portal. They may use the information only to provide those services to us. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Your choices

You may ask us to see, correct, or delete the information you gave us through this website. Call or text (206) 507-2504 or write to us at 2700 Richards Rd, Suite 204A, Bellevue, WA 98005.

Please don't send medical details through the booking form, text, or newsletter sign-up. Before your first visit we'll send a secure intake form for your health history.

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

This policy is provided under Washington's My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373). "Consumer health data" means personal information that identifies your past, present, or future physical or mental health.

Consumer health data we collect

When you book online, the visit type you choose (for example, a consultation about hormones, weight, screening, or skin care) may reveal information about your health. Together with your name, phone, and email, we treat it as consumer health data.

Why we collect it

Only to schedule and manage the appointment you request and to contact you about it. We collect it with your consent, which you give by checking the consent box on the booking form.

Where it comes from

Directly from you, through the booking form.

Who we share it with

The service providers that run our scheduling (our calendar and email provider) and our website host, under contracts that limit their use to providing those services. We do not sell consumer health data. We do not share it with any other third party or affiliate without your separate consent, except as required by law.

Your rights

  • Confirm whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data, and see that data.
  • Get a list of the third parties and affiliates with whom we have shared it.
  • Withdraw your consent.
  • Ask us to delete your consumer health data.

To use any of these rights, call or text (206) 507-2504 or write to us at 2700 Richards Rd, Suite 204A, Bellevue, WA 98005. We will respond within 45 days. If we deny your request, you may appeal by contacting us the same way; if your appeal is denied, you may contact the Washington State Attorney General.

Terms of Use

The content on this website is general information, not medical advice, and does not create a doctor–patient relationship. Always talk with a qualified health professional about your own health. If you have a medical emergency, call 911.

Online booking reserves a time with our clinic. We may contact you to confirm or, if needed, change your appointment. Please give at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule; you can do this with the private link in your confirmation email.

Treatments described on this site are offered only after an evaluation and are not right for everyone. Results vary. Website content may change without notice.

Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this website. It is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA: text has strong color contrast, information is never shown by color alone, every page works with a keyboard and screen reader, and motion is reduced if your device asks for it.

If anything on this site is hard to use, please call or text (206) 507-2504 and we'll help you book or find what you need.
