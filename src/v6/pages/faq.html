Our care
What does "normal is not optimal" mean?

Standard lab reference ranges are built from population averages, which include a lot of people who don't feel their best. We look beyond whether your labs fall within a broad "normal" range and instead aim for the levels where you actually feel and function at your best.

What conditions do you treat?

We specialize in menopause and andropause, weight loss and weight management, PCOS, chronic fatigue syndrome, fibromyalgia, hormone-related depression and mood concerns, longevity and age management, and nutrition coaching.

Is bioidentical hormone therapy safe?

Bioidentical hormone therapy is individualized and monitored closely with regular lab work to keep your levels in a safe, optimal range. Your provider will review your health history and any risk factors before starting treatment.

Membership & payment
Is the initial consultation really free?

Yes. Every member receives a complimentary 20-minute consultation to discuss your symptoms and goals. If you decide to move forward, the next step is a lab draw and full initial consultation to build your personalized plan.

Is membership really free to join?

Yes. There is no cost and no obligation to join. You only pay for the services you choose to receive, such as lab work or ongoing treatment.

Do you accept HSA or FSA cards?

Yes, HSA and FSA cards are accepted for eligible services and products, whether you're visiting in person or booking online.

Visits, booking & products
Do you offer telemedicine appointments?

Yes. Every service we offer is available either in person at our office or entirely by telemedicine. Labs can typically be drawn at a local facility near you if you choose the telemedicine option.

How do I book an appointment?

You can schedule through our booking page, or simply call or text our front desk if that's easier for you.

Can I purchase products without becoming a patient?

Our skin, hair, and hormone-support products are available to order online. For personalized supplement or treatment recommendations, we suggest joining as a member for a free consultation first.
